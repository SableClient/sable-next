Sable is a Matrix client built to enhance the user experience with quality-of-life features, cosmetics, utilities, and sheer usability. Chat with friends and communities on any Matrix server, on your phone, your computer or in the browser.

Features:
- End-to-end encrypted messaging, with encrypted push notification previews decrypted on-device
- Push without Google Play Services, through UnifiedPush
- Voice and video calls over MatrixRTC
- Spaces, threads and forum rooms, with threads in a side panel
- Personas: a different name, avatar and pronouns per message, with PluralKit-style commands
- Customisable app icons, themes, and a configurable GIF provider
- Make your own theme in the theme creator
- Polls and location sharing
- Settings sync across your devices
- Message reporting, forwarding and read-receipt controls
- Presence controls and opt-in crash reporting

Matrix is an open, decentralised and federated chat network. Join a public server, use your community's, or host your own.
